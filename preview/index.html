Your Care Pathway

  1. Consultation

    Time to understand your symptoms, history and what matters most to you.

  2. Assessment & Imaging

    Examination and detailed ultrasound, with further tests only when they are useful.

  3. Individualised Treatment

    Observation, medication, physiotherapy or surgery — chosen together after discussing benefits and risks.

  4. Recovery & Follow-up

    Clear guidance after treatment and planned review of your progress.

Frequently asked questions

General questions about booking, ultrasound, treatment choices, insurance and recovery. Detailed FAQs are on each condition and treatment page.

How do I book a consultation?

You can book by phone, through the hospital appointment desk, or by sending the enquiry form on the Contact page. Please tell us briefly what you would like to discuss so the right appointment length can be arranged.

What should I bring to my first appointment?

Bring your ID and insurance card, previous scan and MRI reports (with images if you have them), operation notes, blood results and a list of current medicines. A short symptom diary is often helpful.

Will I have an ultrasound at my first visit?

A detailed pelvic ultrasound is often part of the assessment when it is clinically relevant, and may be done at the same visit or scheduled separately. Dr. Alphy will explain what the scan can and cannot show.

Will I need surgery?

Not necessarily. Many conditions can be managed with observation, medication, physiotherapy or other non-surgical care. Surgery is discussed when it is likely to help, and the decision is always made together with you.

Is treatment covered by insurance?

Cover depends on your individual policy and insurer. The hospital insurance team can check eligibility and request pre-approval for investigations or procedures where this is required.

How long is recovery after minimally invasive surgery?

Recovery varies with the procedure and the individual. Many patients go home the same day or after one night, but returning to normal activity can take from a few days to several weeks. You will receive advice specific to your operation.
